# Q3 literature and provenance

Primary and supporting sources used for the Q3 analysis:

- International Brain Laboratory et al. (2025), *A brain-wide map of neural
  activity during complex behaviour*, Nature 645. Primary latency benchmark.
- Montijn et al. (2021), ZETA responsiveness method.
- Siegle et al. (2021), visual response hierarchy.
- Groen et al. (2022), visual response dynamics.
- Yang et al. (2023), brain-wide response sequences.
- Posani et al., cortical hierarchy.
- Schartner et al. (2026), brain-wide computation methods.
- Kobak et al. (2016), demixed population analysis.
- Steinmetz et al. (2019), distributed coding.
- IBL standardized-behaviour and reproducible-electrophysiology resources.

Local paper PDFs are intentionally excluded from Git. The analysis notebook
credits the IBL/Neuromatch data-loading foundation separately from Arash
Kanafchian's Q3-specific code.
